🍬 Privacy Policy

Endless Candy Runner · by JP games · Effective date: 9 November 2026

The short version (for players of all ages)

  • 🎮 You don’t need an account, and we never ask for your name, email, photos or contacts.
  • 💾 Your progress (coins, skins, high scores, settings) is saved on your own phone.
  • 🎂 When you first play, we ask the year you were born. We only keep whether you are under 13 or 13+ — never the year itself.
  • 📺 The game shows ads to stay free. If you are under 13 (or we don’t know your age), ads are child-safe: they are not personalised and the ad provider does not receive your phone’s advertising ID.
  • 📊 Players aged 13+ can choose to share anonymous gameplay stats to help improve the game. It is off unless you say yes, and you can turn it off any time in Settings. We never collect these stats from under-13s.
  • 🚫 We never sell your information.
  • ❓ Questions? Ask a parent or carer to email us at alex3218jackson@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Endless Candy Runner (“the game”) is made by JP games, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for how your information is handled in the game. You can contact us at alex3218jackson@gmail.com.

2. Information saved on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, so it can remember your progress. We (the developer) do not receive it:

  • Game progress: coins, unlocked skins and backgrounds, upgrades, high scores, best distances, missions completed and daily reward streaks.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, graphics, “Easy start”, and your stats-sharing choice.
  • Your age group (“under 13” or “13 and over”), worked out from the birth year you choose. The birth year itself is not stored.
  • A record of which version of small game tests you are seeing (for example, the wording on a button) — see section 5.

This information stays on your device and is deleted if you uninstall the game. You can also reset it by clearing the game’s data in your phone’s settings.

3. The age question

The first time you play, the game asks for your birth year. We ask this so we can keep younger players safe: it decides which kind of ads you see and whether you are offered optional stats-sharing. We only keep the result (under 13 / 13+). If you haven’t answered, the game treats you as under 13.

4. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads using Google AdMob, provided by Google. Ads appear when you choose to watch them for a reward (for example, to revive or double your coins), and — for players aged 13+ only — occasionally between runs.

Players under 13 (and players whose age we don’t know)

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed, following the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and as being from a user under the age of consent (UK/EU).
  • Ads are not personalised and are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
  • Google does not receive the device’s advertising ID from these requests.
  • Google may still process limited technical information needed to show an ad and to prevent fraud (such as the IP address, device type and operating system), as described in Google’s policies.
  • Players under 13 never see between-run (interstitial) ads.

Players aged 13 and over

  • Google may collect information such as your device’s advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, Google’s consent message will ask whether ads can be personalised. You can change your choice through your device’s settings or the consent options in the game, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a “parental guidance” rating or below.

Google acts independently when handling this information. Please see How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Optional gameplay stats (13+ only, off unless you say yes)

Players aged 13 and over are asked once whether they would like to share anonymous gameplay stats to help us improve the game. The answer is no unless you choose “Yes”, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Share stats. These stats are never collected from players under 13 or from players whose age we don’t know.

If you say yes, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to receive:

  • Gameplay events — for example, when a run starts and ends, how long it lasted, its score, which game mode was played, and whether rewards or revives were used.
  • Which version of a small test you are seeing — for example, the wording on a revive button or the coin amounts in a reward — so we can see which works better. These tests never change how hard the game is or whether a run is fair.
  • Technical information collected by Firebase, such as a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version and approximate country.

These stats do not include your name, email or contacts, and we do not use them to identify you. We keep them for no longer than 14 months. See Firebase’s privacy information.

7. Children

The game is designed for players of all ages, and we take children’s privacy seriously. We follow Google Play’s Families policy, the UK Information Commissioner’s Office Age Appropriate Design Code (the Children’s Code) and COPPA. In particular:

  • We ask for age in a neutral way and treat anyone under 13 — or whose age we don’t know — as a child.
  • Children see only non-personalised, child-safe ads, and the advertising ID is not sent.
  • We never collect gameplay stats from children.
  • We never ask children for personal information, and the game has no chat or social features.

If you are a parent or carer and have questions, or want us to help remove information, please contact us at alex3218jackson@gmail.com.

8. Sharing your information

We do not sell or rent your information. Information is only handled by Google as described in sections 4 and 5. We may also share information if the law requires it.

9. International transfers

Google may process information on servers outside the UK and European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google uses legal safeguards for these transfers, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension).

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time (for stats, use Settings → Share stats). Because almost everything is stored only on your device, the quickest way to delete it is to uninstall the game or clear its data. For anything else, email alex3218jackson@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO), or to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

Your game data is stored using your device’s own app storage. Information sent to Google is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep the information we handle to a minimum.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date at the top. If a change affects what we collect from players, we will let you know in the game.
